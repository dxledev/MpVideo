# MpVideo interface

The video is the primary surface. Flat, theme-derived chrome frames it with a compact header, a thin timeline and a consistent transport row. The queue shares the playback area when opened. A simple geometric play motif supplies the app identity; all control marks are drawn with the current foreground color.

Use one native sans-serif family, a restrained type scale, 6–8 px control radii, 12–24 px spacing, visible focus borders, and readable disabled states. Frequent playback operations respond immediately. No ornamental motion or gradients.

All dialogs are child widgets over a reduced blurred snapshot, following the efficient one-time blur technique of the reference window. Their contents scroll within the window and background interaction is disabled until dismissal. The snapshot is released when closed.
