# MpVideo

A portable, standalone C++ video player for Linux, powered by libmpv with a Qt interface. The main task is opening and watching local media or streams. Users can queue files, choose audio and subtitle tracks, seek, adjust volume and playback speed, and use fullscreen.

Requirements supplied by the project brief: no GTK, no gradients, a resizable Wayland window, live CLI color theming persisted as JSON, colorable vector controls, and dialogs embedded within the app window. Theme colors accept Qt alpha-first hex and Qt.rgba expressions.

Assumed direction: a distraction-free Operate interface with the video taking most of the window, restrained flat chrome, immediate keyboard controls, and a queue that appears only when needed.
